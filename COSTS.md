# Costs

The film cost nothing to make. This document records what was measured, what the free tiers actually allowed, and what the same usage would cost at list prices.

## How this is measured

- Every model call writes one line to `runs.jsonl` with its latency and its cost in the provider's own unit: tokens for Gemini, neurons for Cloudflare, GPU seconds for Kaggle.
- `make costs` reads that file and rebuilds the "Measured" section below. Nothing in it is typed by hand.
- Prices are applied afterwards from [pipeline/pricing.json](pipeline/pricing.json), so a corrected price never means rewriting the log.

Two limits on accuracy:

- **Cloudflare neurons are estimates.** The API does not return usage per call. We compute it from Cloudflare's published price for `flux-2-klein-4b`: 26.05 neurons per 512×512 output tile and 5.37 per input tile.
- **Kaggle GPU time has two meanings.** Session time is what counts against the weekly quota and includes installing libraries and downloading the model. Generating time is the sum of the per-clip and per-track timings. Both are reported.

## Free-tier limits

The brief asks us to check each limit and write down what we actually found.

| Provider | What the brief says | What we found on 26 November 2026 | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemini API, text | "Free tier, Ethiopia supported" | `gemini-3.8-flash` and `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` are free. Google publishes no request limits; they are shown per project on the rate-limit dashboard. Our project's limits: **to be read from the dashboard**. | [pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing), [rate limits](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/rate-limits) |
| Gemini API, speech | "Free tier, 130+ languages incl. Amharic" | `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` and `gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts` are free. Amharic is supported by both. No request limit is published. Our project's limits: **to be read from the dashboard**. | same |
| Gemini daily reset | not stated | Midnight Pacific time, which is 11:00 East Africa Time. Limits apply per project. | rate limits page |
| Cloudflare Workers AI | "roughly 50 to 170 images" a day | 10,000 neurons a day across all models, reset at 00:00 UTC (03:00 East Africa Time). That is about 95 images at 1024×1024 with `flux-2-klein-4b`, about 91 with one reference image, and fewer at larger sizes. | [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/) |
| Kaggle GPU | "about 30 GPU hours a week" | A batch job may run for 12 hours and write 20 GB. We could not confirm the weekly hours from Kaggle's own pages. Our account's quota: **to be read from the account page**. | [notebooks docs](https://www.kaggle.com/docs/notebooks) |

Free Gemini calls come with a condition: Google's pricing page says free-tier content is used to improve its products. See [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md).
